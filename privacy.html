Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

TOE Connect is a free directory of Black-owned businesses in Wisconsin, operated by Time of Essence Global Group, LLC ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When a business owner submits a listing, we collect what is on the submission form:

  • Business name, category, short description, and optional full bio
  • Street address, city, and ZIP code, and whether the address should be shown or kept private
  • Hours of operation (optional)
  • Contact email, phone number, website or social link, booking link, and owner video link (some optional)
  • A logo or photo, if provided to us
  • License or certification numbers, where requested
  • Your confirmations about ownership (for example, majority Black-owned or majority women-owned)
  • Any optional contribution amount you indicate on the form

People who visit the directory do not need an account and are not asked for personal information to browse or search. If you send us a message through the contact form, we collect your name, email address, topic, and message so we can reply. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record basic technical information, such as IP address and browser type, in its server logs.

What is public and what stays private

Shown publicly on your listing: business name, category, descriptions, city, hours, phone number (if you provide one), website, booking link, owner video, logo or photo, and any verification badges you earn. Your street address is shown only if you choose to show it. Your email is shown only if you choose to show it.

Not shown publicly: your email by default, license and certification numbers, your street address if you choose private or none, and our internal review notes.

How we use information

  • To review, publish, and maintain listings in the directory
  • To contact you about the status of your listing or questions about it
  • To confirm information needed for verification badges
  • To operate, secure, and improve the directory
  • To respond to messages and requests

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We use a small number of service providers to run the directory, and they process information on our behalf:

  • Airtable, where listing information is stored
  • Netlify and GitHub, which host and deliver the website. Netlify also receives contact form messages and forwards them to us by email
  • Square, which processes optional contributions on its own secure page. We do not receive or store your card number
  • YouTube and Vimeo, which play owner and founder videos if a video is shown. TikTok, Instagram and Facebook videos load only after you click Play, and those companies may then collect information about your visit under their own policies
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the typefaces used on the site

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public.

Links to other websites

Listings may link to outside websites, including booking pages, and to videos. When you book or buy through a business's own link, you are dealing with that business and its tools, and its own privacy practices apply, not this policy.

Cookies and browser storage

The site may store files in your browser, such as a service worker that helps pages load faster. We do not currently use advertising or cross-site tracking tools on this site.

Keeping and removing information

We keep listing information while a listing is active. You can ask us at any time to update or remove your listing, or to change whether your address or email is shown, by contacting us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time. Copies may remain in backups for a short period after removal.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, including keeping private details off the public site. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

TOE Connect is intended for adults and business owners. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
