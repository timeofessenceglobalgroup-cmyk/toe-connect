About TOE Connect

Connecting Wisconsin to Black-owned businesses

TOE Connect is a free directory that helps people across Wisconsin find, trust, and support Black-owned businesses, and gives every owner a place to be found.

Who we are

TOE Connect is a project of Time of Essence Global Group, LLC, a family of businesses based in Southeast Wisconsin. We built it because Black-owned businesses in our state deserve to be easy to find, whether they are brand new or have served their community for years.

What we do

  • A free, searchable directory. Find businesses by category, by location, and by how they serve you: in person, online, or by traveling to you.
  • Reviewed listings. Every listing is reviewed before it goes live, and verification badges show which checks a business has passed. See how badges work.
  • Easy ways to connect. Each listing can show a phone number, website, hours, directions, and a Book now button for businesses that take online bookings.
  • Resources for owners. Tips for Business Owners, Free Tools & Resources, and Community Events.

Our mission

To make Black-owned businesses in Wisconsin easy to find, and to give every owner, at any stage, a free place to be seen.

Our vision

A Wisconsin where Black-owned businesses are visible, trusted, and thriving, and where every community member can find and support them with confidence.

What we stand for

  • Free, always. Listing costs nothing, for new and established businesses alike. Contributions are optional, are never required, and never affect a listing.
  • Trust through review. We check listings before they appear and explain what our badges mean.
  • Community first. We build this directory for the people who use it and the owners who fill it.
  • Every stage welcome. Home-based, mobile, online, or storefront: if you are a majority Black-owned Wisconsin business, you belong here.
