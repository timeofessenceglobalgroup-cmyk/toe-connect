Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Listing is free, with no membership and no monthly fee. A contribution at the end of the form is completely optional, is never required, and has no effect on your listing.

Who can be listed?

Majority Black-owned businesses based in Wisconsin. You can also serve clients outside the state or online. Home-based and mobile businesses are welcome.

What happens after I submit?

Every listing is reviewed before it goes live, usually within a few days. We may email you if we have a question. Once approved, your business appears in the directory.

What will be shown publicly?

Your business name, category, descriptions, city, hours, website, booking link, owner video, and phone number if you provide one. Your street address is shown only if you choose to show it. Your email stays private unless you choose to show it, and license or certification numbers are never shown.

I work from home or travel to clients. Can I still list?

Yes. In the Location section, choose "home-based" to keep your street address private, or "mobile or online only" if there is no address to show. Your city is still shown so people know where you serve.

Do I need a license number?

For childcare, home health, medical and dental, and mental health listings, yes. It earns your listing a verified badge. Other categories can leave it blank.

What are the badges?

Badges show which checks a listing has passed, such as a verified business, a verified license, or a reviewed ownership confirmation. See Verification & Badges for the details.

What is the booking link for?

If customers can book online, paste your booking page link (Calendly, Square, Vagaro, your own site, and so on) and a "Book now" button will appear on your listing. Bookings and notifications go through your own booking tool.

How do I update or remove my listing later?

Send us a message through the contact form or email toeconnect4@gmail.com and we will take care of it.

Still have a question? Send us a message before you submit. We are happy to help.
