For business owners just getting started

Free Tools & Resources

A short list of genuinely free tools worth knowing about — no trial periods, no hidden catches, just things that work while you're getting your business off the ground.

Video meetings

Alternatives to Zoom's time-limited free tier.
Google Meet
Free with any Google account, no time limit on 1-on-1 calls, generous limits on group calls.
Visit →
Whereby
No account needed for guests to join — good for client meetings where you don't want to ask them to sign up for anything.
Visit →
Jitsi Meet
Completely free, no account required for anyone, no time limits.
Visit →

Scheduling

Calendly
Free tier covers one event type — good enough for a simple "book a call with me" link.
Visit →

Invoicing & bookkeeping

Wave
Genuinely free invoicing and double-entry bookkeeping — not a trial, an actual free plan.
Visit →
Square Invoices
Free to send invoices; Square only takes a fee when a client actually pays through it.
Visit →

Design & marketing

Canva
Free tier is genuinely useful for flyers, social posts, and simple logos — no design experience needed.
Visit →
Mailchimp
Free tier covers a small email list — a real way to start building a customer list without paying.
Visit →

Funding & mentorship

Wisconsin-specific and free.
WWBIC — Wisconsin Women's Business Initiative Corporation
Free and low-cost business coaching and loan programs, open to all small business owners in Wisconsin, not just women-owned businesses.
Visit →
SCORE
Free one-on-one mentoring from experienced business volunteers, with a Milwaukee chapter.
Visit →
