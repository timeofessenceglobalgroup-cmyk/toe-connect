Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for people looking for a business and for owners thinking about listing.

About the directory
What is TOE Connect?

A free directory of Black-owned businesses in Wisconsin, run by Time of Essence Global Group, LLC. You can learn more on our About page.

How do I find a business?

Search by name or keyword, or filter by category, city or ZIP, and how a business serves you (in person, also remotely, or mobile). You can sort the results and switch between a grid and a list.

Do I have to pay to use the directory?

No. Browsing and searching are free for everyone.

How do I book a business?

If a business takes online bookings, its listing shows a Book now button that opens its own booking page. Otherwise, use the phone number or website on its listing. Bookings and payments are between you and the business.

What do the badges mean?

Badges show which checks a listing has passed, such as a verified business, a verified license, or a reviewed ownership confirmation. See Verification & Badges for details. A badge is not a guarantee of any business or its services.

Something on a listing looks wrong. What should I do?

Please send us a message with the business name and what you noticed, and we will look into it.

For business owners
Is it really free to list my business?

Yes. Listing is free, with no membership and no monthly fee. A contribution at the end of the form is completely optional, is never required, and has no effect on your listing.

Who can be listed?

Majority Black-owned businesses based in Wisconsin. You can also serve clients outside the state or online. Home-based and mobile businesses are welcome.

What happens after I submit?

Every listing is reviewed before it goes live, usually within a few days. We may email you if we have a question. Once approved, your business appears in the directory.

What will be shown publicly?

Your business name, category, descriptions, city, hours, website, booking link, owner video, and phone number if you provide one. Your street address is shown only if you choose to show it. Your email stays private unless you choose to show it, and license or certification numbers are never shown.

I work from home or travel to clients. Can I still list?

Yes. In the Location section of the form, choose "home-based" to keep your street address private, or "mobile or online only" if there is no address to show. Your city is still shown so people know where you serve.

Do I need a website?

No. A website or social link is optional. You can list with just a phone number, an email, or a booking link.

Do I need a license number?

For childcare, home health, medical and dental, and mental health listings, yes. It earns your listing a verified badge. Other categories can leave it blank.

What is the booking link for?

If customers can book online (Calendly, Square, Vagaro, your own site, and so on), paste your booking page link and a "Book now" button will appear on your listing. Bookings and notifications go through your own booking tool.

What is the owner video?

An optional 30-second introduction to you and your business. Upload it to YouTube (set to "Unlisted") or Vimeo for free, then paste the link into the form.

How do I update or remove my listing?

There are no accounts to manage. Send us a message through the contact form or email toeconnect4@gmail.com, and we will take care of it.

Contributions and privacy
What does an optional contribution do?

It helps keep the directory free for the next business owner. It is never required, has no effect on how a listing is reviewed or shown, and is processed by Square on its own secure page. Contributions are not tax-deductible.

Is TOE Connect a nonprofit?

No. TOE Connect is a project of Time of Essence Global Group, LLC, which is not a registered nonprofit.

How is my information used?

To review and publish your listing and to contact you about it. We do not sell personal information. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use for the details.
