Support TOE Connect

Listings on TOE Connect are always free. If you'd like to help keep the directory running, a voluntary contribution is welcome. You don't need to be listed to give, and giving is never required to be listed.

What to know

  • A contribution is voluntary. It is not a membership and it is not a tax-deductible donation.
  • It does not add a listing, a badge, or any change to how a business appears in the directory.
  • Every listing is reviewed the same way, whether or not the owner contributes.
  • You choose the amount.
  • Contributions are non-refundable.

Where the money goes

Contributions help cover what it takes to keep TOE Connect online and growing: hosting, the domain, site upkeep, and outreach to Wisconsin Black-owned businesses.
